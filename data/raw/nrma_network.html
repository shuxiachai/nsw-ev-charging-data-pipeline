NRMA Electric charging network

Whether you charge at home or rely on public chargers, the NRMA Electric charging network allows you to go further.

Location-based variable pricing is now live across all chargers on the network, please refer to the My NRMA app to view charger-specific pricing. Even better, members always enjoy a discount on charging.
