Old Bega Hospital - Location

The Old Bega Hospital Reserve is at 3 Corkhill Place (off Newtown Road, formerly the Princes Highway), Bega, NSW 2550, on the far south coast of New South Wales and the southern outskirts of Bega, about 430 km south of Sydney.

It is on the road into Bega near the southern entry from the Princes Highway bypass, on the coastal side, about 2km from Bega's commercial centre.

The land on which the Hospital stands is Lot 296 in Deposited Plan 728021, and was formerly part of the Bega Permanent Common.

 

The site covers about 1.603 hectares (4 acres).

Above: The deposited plan as registered 12 April 1989. Click on the image to see the details. The Old Bega Hospital Reserve is lot 296, towards the top left on page 1, and in more detail on page 5.

Below: An older plan, reflecting arrangements in the second half of the 1950s, with the 1989 changes annotated by hand. The Old Hospital site is lot 296 in this one too.
