FREE WI-FI

Customers at Narellan Town Centre can now enjoy the benefits of free Wi-Fi. To join the free Wi-Fi Service simply select ‘@Narellan Town Centre Free Wi-Fi’ from your list of available Wi-Fi networks, enter your details and select ‘Connect to Free Wi-Fi’

By connecting to the Wi-Fi you agree to the ‘Terms and Conditions of Use’ and Narellan Town Centre’s ‘Privacy Policy’.

 

Electronic Vehicle Charging

Evie provides convenient, fast charging powered by 100% renewable energy wherever you work, shop or play.

Charge your EV with up to 150kms of range in 15 mins with Evie fast charging. Located on South Side of the centre carpark – enter off Queen Street and take the first left as you enter the carpark.

Download the Evie app to be ready to charge and to explore the full Evie network.

 

Lost & Found

If you have lost or found any property whilst visiting Narellan Town Centre, please either visit our Customer Service Desk, Centre Management Office or call    02 4647 4123 .

 

 

 

Lifts

For easy access to the centre, lifts are positioned in the following locations:

South Building:

  • Opposite Lowes: Access to Level One, Ground Level Carpark & Rooftop Carpark
  • South Side Plaza: Access to Level One & South Side Plaza (which also provides access to the Ground Level Carpark)
  • United Cinemas: Access to Level One, United Cinemas & Ground Level Carpark

North Building:

  • Near Commercial Centre & Coles: Access to Level One, Mezzanine Level Carpark & Restaurant Plaza (which also provides access to the Ground Level Carpark)
  • Near Harry Hartog Bookseller & Kmart: Access to Level One, Mezzanine Level Carpark & Restaurant Plaza (which also provides access to the Ground Level Carpark)

Please note that LEVEL ONE refers to the retail level. Shops located on the Ground Level on the South Side are best accessed using the United Cinemas lift.
