Tesla EV Charging Station – Campbelltown Catholic Club

3 years ago | by coaststudios

Campbelltown Catholic Club has installed 12 Tesla Superchargers. They are available available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, up to 250kW and have CCS Compatibility.

The Catholic Club is a place to rest and refresh and more details are here: Campbelltown Catholic Club – A Caring Community of Fun (cathclub.com.au).

For more informatio on the Tesla’s Supercharger network visit here: Tesla/Supercharger
