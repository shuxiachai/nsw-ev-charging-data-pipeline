Location

Getting Here

Located in the vibrant inner west suburb of Leichhardt, MarketPlace Leichhardt offers excellent connectivity and convenience. Positioned on Flood Street with easy access to major arterial roads, the centre provides seamless connections to Sydney CBD and surrounding inner west communities. With convenient parking facilities and proximity to public transport networks, MarketPlace Leichhardt serves as an accessible retail destination for locals and visitors from across the inner west corridor.

Parking

Carpark hours

6:30 AM - 10:30 PM daily

Parking at Marketplace Leichhardt is FREE.

We have thirteen mobility impaired car parking spaces and nine designated Parents with Prams car parking spaces conveniently located near the main entrances to the Centre.

*Hours may vary on public holidays.

*A Land and Environment Court Order prohibits car park access outside of these hours.

Car

MarketPlace Leichhardt is located 8 kilometres west of Sydney CBD on the corner of Marion and Flood Streets, with convenient free parking available for customers.

Parking at MarketPlace Leichhardt is FREE. We have thirteen mobility impaired car parking spaces and nine designated Parents with Prams car parking spaces conveniently located near the main entrances to the Centre.

6:30 AM - 10:30 PM

Bus

Marketplace Leichhardt is serviced by multiple bus routes including 437 to Five Dock, 438N and 438X to Abbotsford, and 469 via Glebe. Bus stops are located on Flood Street.

For further details visit transportnsw.info or call 13 15 00.

Train

MarketPlace Leichhardt is a 20-minute walk from Lewisham train station, which operates on the T2 Inner West and South Line providing connections to Sydney CBD, Campbelltown and other destinations across the network.

For timetables and further details visit transportnsw.info or call 13 15 00.

Taxi

Taxi services are available from the dedicated taxi stand located on Flood Street near BWS.

Bicycle

MarketPlace Leichhardt is accessible via Inner West Council bicycle routes including shared paths, off-road and on-road mixed traffic routes.

Bike racks are located on Flood Street near the Marion Street entrance and in the staff car park. For bicycle route maps visit Inner West Council.

Lightrail

MarketPlace Leichhardt is 400 metres from Marion Street light rail station, with frequent services to Dulwich Hill and Central Station.

For timetables and fare information visit transportnsw.info or call 13 15 00.

Amenities

Centre Services

MarketPlace Leichhardt offers a range of services and amenities designed to make your visit comfortable, convenient, and accessible for everyone.

Justice of the Peace

The Justice of the Peace Service, is a complimentary service offered by the Department of Justice and the Inner West Council that operates from the Community Room directly behind the Medical Centre, the hours for this service are:

Thursday

2 PM - 4 PM

Saturday

10 AM - 12 NOON

*No appointment is necessary.

Parent’s Room

For parents or minders shopping with children, our parent room provides a clean, comfortable and convenient space where the whole family is welcome. We provide a microwave, change tables and quiet feeding areas.

Parents with Prams Parking

Car parks dedicated to parents with prams are located on the East rooftop car park Centre entrance and the basement car park Centre entrance.

Complimentary Wheelchair Hire

To ensure your shopping experience is comfortable, MarketPlace Leichhardt offers customers complimentary wheelchair hire. See the friendly staff at Centre Management. Hire conditions apply.

ATMs

MarketPlace Leichhardt has a range of automatic teller machines (ATMs) including Commonwealth Bank, ANZ, and ATMX.

EV Chargers

Four EV Chargers are located in the basement carpark for your convenience.

Community

Community Room

Leichhardt Council has a Community Meeting Room at MarketPlace Leichhardt which is available for hire. For details and instructions on hire please visit leichhardt.nsw.gov.au

Sustainability Initiatives

MarketPlace Leichhardt, has achieved a 5 Star NABERS Energy rating (the Centre uses 100% GreenPower) and a 4 Star NABERS rating for water consumption.

MarketPlace Leichhardt began its quest to attain NABERS accreditation in 2007, systematically introducing energy and water saving initiatives – of which have cumulatively reduced energy consumption by 54.20%.
