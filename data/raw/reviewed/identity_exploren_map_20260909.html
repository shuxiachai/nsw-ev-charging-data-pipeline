FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about finding and using EV chargers on the Exploren network.

Use the map on this page or open the Exploren app and allow location access. You'll see nearby chargers, their connector types and whether they're currently available. You can filter by location and check live status before you set off, so you're never guessing whether a bay is free.

Exploren connects drivers to thousands of charge points across Australia and New Zealand, and the network grows every month as new businesses, councils and property owners join. Because we're charger agnostic and OCPP compliant, we work with more than 50 charger brands.

The app is the easiest way to find EV charging stations, check availability and track your session, but you can also start a charge by scanning the QR code on the charger itself. Both options let you pay securely without a subscription.

Pricing is set by the owner of each charger, not by Exploren, so rates vary by location. You'll always see the tariff for a station in the app before you begin, and you'll get a full session summary showing energy used, duration and cost.
