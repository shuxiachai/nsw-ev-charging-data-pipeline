ALL WELCOME! TUTTI BENVENUTO!

Our unique Historic New Italy Museum complex and facilities stand on acreage that was once part of the original New Italy Settlement dating back centuries.

This historical site has been developed and is maintained by volunteering descendants and friends of the some 200 Italian survivors of the disastrous Marquis de Ray’s expedition / settlement attempt in the South West Pacific of 1879-1881. These destitute people settled here in 1882 and established a thriving Italian speaking village in the Australian bush.

The Museum commemorates the stories of the expeditioners and those families who established the vibrant community of New Italy, carved out of the poor bushland of the region.

We are located on the Pacific Highway in the Northern Rivers region of New South Wales, between Byron Bay and Grafton. We are open seven days and entry is free. New Italy is a great place to rest, charge your EV, enjoy lunch or an excellent coffee, shop for truly unique gifts, let your four legged friends play in our fenced off-leash dog park and immerse yourself in a truly memorable and historic Italian experience. 🇮🇹

HALL HIRE

LOOKING FOR AN AFFORDABLE VENUE FOR YOUR NEXT EVENT OR FUNCTION?

The New Italy Hall is now available for hire! Offering an easily accessible, highway adjacent location, with great facilities and plenty of parking, the New Italy Hall is an attractive option for your next event or function. The BYO opportunity makes our hall a particularly affordable solution.

A blank canvas, the hall seats approximately 400, or alternatively, up to 220 seated at tables. Facilities in the hall include a small bar area and a raised stage with a wall mounted large flat screen TV at the southern end of the building. EV SUPERCHARGER

Supercharged news for Historic New Italy!

The award winning EV charging facility at Historic New Italy is open and the driver feedback received so far is overwhelmingly positive! 

This impressive installation features 11 individual charging stations shaded by the solar array canopy and one pull-through parking for EVs towing trailers.  It also features a convenient tap-and-go credit card payment option.

It is a Tesla Supercharger but it's not just for Teslas as the chargers are equipped with CCS2 plugs so can charge a wide range of compatible EVs.

It's the perfect place along the M1 to stop for a break, a cuppa, a visit to the museum, a wonder through the shops and of course - a charge! 

Winning! The Australian Electric Vehicle Association (AEVA) has chosen the Tesla Supercharger station at Historic New Italy in northern New South Wales as its 2025 Charging Station of the Year!

This prestigious title is awarded to the most reliable, convenient, well kept, and thoughtfully designed public EV charging station in the country.

The New Italy facility, located off the Pacific Motorway in northern NSW, offers a "very high level of amenity for EV users", AEVA says
