GETTING HERE

BY CAR - FREE UNLIMITED PARKING

Narellan Town Centre provides over 3,000 car spaces split across both the North and South sides of the centre.

 

North Building Carpark

Ground Level & Mezzanine Level

Location Best Parking For:
Eastern End - Retail Level Coles, Fresh Food, Commercial Centre, Restaurant Plaza, Banking Precinct
Western End Kmart, H&M, Restaurant Plaza

 

South Building Carpark

Ground Level Retail Level, & Rooftop

Location Best Parking For:
Eastern End – Retail Level & Rooftop carparks Woolworths, Fresh Food
Western End – Ground Level carpark Big W, JB Hi Fi, Food Court, United Cinemas, Target

For further details on Narellan Town Centre carparks contact Centre Management.

BY BUS

Local buses operate regularly around the Narellan area from key locations including Campelltown, Camden and Picton. Local buses drop off and pick up passengers in two locations in close proximity to the centre:

  • Somerset Avenue (near Woolworths entrance)
  • Elyard Street (near United Cinemas and Food Court)

To plan your bus trip to Narellan Town Centre click here.

By Bicycle

Bicycle racks are positioned at the following locations at Narellan Town Centre:

  • North Building: Near the Restaurant Plaza (underneath the retail bridge)
  • South Building: Somerset Entrance (near Medical Centre)
  • South Building: South Side Plaza
  • South Building: Lower Ground entrance (Queen Street) next to Australia Post
