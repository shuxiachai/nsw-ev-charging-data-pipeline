From 1 September
Where are they located?

Who can use them?
Open to all CCS2 compatible EVs (≈99% of EVs in Australia), not just Tesla
How fast are they?
Up to 250kW – that’s 282km of range in 15 minutes
How much does it cost?
Pricing is shown in the Tesla App (required to charge)
