Parraween Street car park, Cremorne - Council car parks

Address
Parraween Street, Cremorne, 2090
Location
Opening hours
24 hours, 7 days a week
Parking restrictions

Three-hours of free parking is available:

  • Monday to Friday - 8am to 6pm
  • Saturday - 8am to 12 noon.

All other hours are unrestricted.

Accessible spaces
One
Electric charging bays

Two dual 75kW DC fast chargers (four charging bays) operated by Evie. For support, contact Evie on 1300 463 843.

Apply for a permanent car space

For permanent residential and commercial parking space availability, please email council@northsydney.nsw.gov.au
