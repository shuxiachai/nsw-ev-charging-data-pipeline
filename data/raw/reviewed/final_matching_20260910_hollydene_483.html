Hollydene Estate

A vineyard with a family focus that welcomes families to our expansive property. Families are encouraged to enjoy the large level lawn and meet the farm animals . With breath-taking views over the Wollemi National Park there is plenty of room to relax, enjoy a glass of wine and watch the children play. 

Less than an hours scenic drive from the heart of Pokolbin. With it's beautiful views and stunning setting Hollydene Estate is worth the trip! 

Hollydene Estate Cellar Door offers a large selection of wines in a rustic, modern setting complete with historic wooden beams from the original Bowman’s bridge that once crossed the nearby Hunter River.

Wine tastings are given at at the grape-embossed bar and visitors can also recline on comfortable couches to enjoy spectacular views over Coolmore Thoroughbred horse stud and Wollemi National Park.

Hollydene Estate wines are made of grapes from three of the oldest vineyards in the Upper Hunter. Varieties include typical Hunter stalwarts like Semillon, Chardonnay and Shiraz as well as lesser-known varieties like Gewürztraminer, Sangiovese and Tempranillo.

Enjoy a meal at the estate's restaurant Vines at Hollydene, serving a delicious paddock to plate seasonal menu.

Breakfast and Lunch 8.30am - 3pm Wednesday to Sunday.

Cellar Door is open Daily 10am -4pm (Monday and Tuesday offering coffee cake antipasto toasties and wine only)
