Heatherbrae

Located on the Pacific Highway at Heatherbrae, right at the roundabout to Raymond Terrace near Newcastle. Being just 2 hours north of Sydney, it is the perfect rest stop on the drive north or south. A federation style bakery with a warm, welcoming atmosphere. Our large dining and alfresco areas can cater for over 120 patrons. Two large car parks are on site with dedicated parking areas for caravans and trailers. More than just a pie shop, at Heatherbrae’s Pies we offer a full sit down or take away meal. There is a large selection of side dishes as well as quality coffee and beverages.
